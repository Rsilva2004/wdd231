NP Membership

NP Membership is designed for non-profit organizations and has no membership fee.

  • Free membership
  • Networking opportunities
  • Access to chamber events
  • Business resources and training

Bronze Membership

Bronze Membership provides essential benefits for small and growing businesses.

  • Access to chamber events
  • Networking opportunities
  • Business training
  • Member discounts

Silver Membership

Silver Membership offers additional promotional and business development opportunities.

  • All Bronze benefits
  • Event discounts
  • Additional business training
  • Promotional opportunities
  • Featured advertising opportunities

Gold Membership

Gold Membership provides premium benefits and increased visibility for established businesses.

  • All Silver benefits
  • Premium advertising opportunities
  • Home page spotlight opportunities
  • Priority event promotion
  • Premium networking opportunities
